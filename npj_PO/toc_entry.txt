PREDICT-AML integrates gene expression, somatic mutations, pathway activity, and molecular drug representations into a single multi-omics framework for personalized AML drug response prediction. Trained on BeatAML and validated on two independent international cohorts, it demonstrates that patient-stratified cross-validation is the clinically appropriate benchmark, and identifies monocyte-like cell-state enrichment and BCL6 expression as recurrent determinants of multi-drug sensitivity.
